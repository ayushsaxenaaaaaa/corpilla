About Sunnah Hospital

A Surgical & Medical Cancer Center built on three values: Hope, Healing and Humanity.

Hospital Profile

Dedicated to the care of people with cancer

Sunnah Hospital – Cancer Research Center brings Surgical Oncology, Medical Oncology and cancer research together in one hospital.

We know that a cancer diagnosis affects the whole family. Patients need skilled treatment, and they also need honest guidance, emotional support, the right nutrition and help with recovery. Our hospital is organised to provide all of these together.

Every patient is treated as an individual. Our doctors take time to explain the diagnosis and the choices available, so that families can decide with confidence.

Our Vision

To be a trusted centre for cancer care and research, where every patient is treated with skill and dignity.

Our Mission
  • To provide specialised surgical and medical cancer treatment based on sound medical evidence.
  • To guide patients and families honestly, and to help them avoid unnecessary investigations and expenses.
  • To care for the whole person through counselling, nutrition, rehabilitation and family education.
  • To contribute to cancer research and awareness in the community we serve.
Our Values

Hope. Healing. Humanity.

HOPE

We meet every patient with honesty and optimism. Whatever the stage of illness, there is always something we can do to help.

HEALING

Healing means more than treating a tumour. It includes the body, the mind and the family around the patient.

HUMANITY

Every person who comes to us is treated with respect, compassion and dignity.

Our Unique Approach

Beyond cancer treatment

Along with specialised Surgical & Medical Oncology, our integrated patient-support approach focuses on five areas.

Right Guidance

Treatment navigation and help with informed decisions, including guidance to avoid unnecessary investigations and expenses.

Psychological Support

Professional counselling and emotional support for patients and their families.

Specialized Nutrition

A dedicated oncology dietitian for personalised nutrition during chemotherapy, after surgery and throughout recovery.

Recovery & Rehabilitation

Post-treatment recovery guidance and supportive care.

Lifestyle & Wellness

Long-term nutrition, lifestyle guidance and family education.

Our Promise

We don't just treat cancer. We support the patient, strengthen the family and guide them through every step of the journey.
