About Us

Sunnah Cancer Research Center: a dedicated unit of Sunnah Hospital, Akola.

Hospital Profile

Cancer care for the people of Vidarbha

Sunnah Cancer Research Center is a dedicated unit of Sunnah Hospital, committed to providing comprehensive, compassionate and affordable cancer care to the people of Vidarbha.

We believe that every patient deserves not just treatment, but emotional strength, dignity and support throughout their journey.

Our approach combines:

  • Advanced medical science
  • Skilled oncology specialists
  • Deep human compassion
Meet Our Doctors
Our Vision

To be a trusted centre for cancer care and research, where every patient is treated with skill and dignity.

Our Mission
  • To provide specialised surgical and medical cancer treatment based on sound medical evidence.
  • To guide patients and families honestly, and to help them avoid unnecessary investigations and expenses.
  • To care for the whole person through counselling, nutrition, rehabilitation and family education.
  • To contribute to cancer research and awareness in the community we serve.
