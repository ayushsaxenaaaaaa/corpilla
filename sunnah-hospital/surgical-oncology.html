Overview

Surgery planned around the whole patient

For many solid tumors, surgery offers the best chance of cure. Our surgical oncologists plan every operation with medical and radiation oncologists, so surgery fits into one complete treatment plan.

Wherever possible we use minimally invasive techniques: smaller cuts, less pain, shorter hospital stays and faster recovery. When larger surgery is needed, our reconstructive surgeons restore form and function in the same operation.

Frequently asked questions

Is keyhole (laparoscopic) surgery as effective for cancer?

For suitable patients, yes. Studies show outcomes similar to open surgery, with less pain and faster recovery. Your surgeon will tell you whether it suits your case.

How long will I stay in hospital?

It depends on the operation. Most patients stay 2 to 5 days. Major GI or head and neck surgery may need 7 to 10 days.

Will I need chemotherapy or radiation after surgery?

That depends on your final pathology report. The tumor board reviews it and recommends any further treatment.
