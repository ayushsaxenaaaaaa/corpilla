Our Unique Approach

Care for the patient and the family

At Sunnah Hospital, we believe cancer care goes beyond medical treatment.

A cancer diagnosis can bring fear, confusion and emotional stress to both patients and their families. Our commitment is to provide the right guidance, compassionate support and comprehensive care throughout their cancer journey.

Integrated Patient Support

Five ways we support you

Right Guidance

Treatment navigation and help with informed decisions, including guidance to avoid unnecessary investigations and expenses.

Psychological Support

Professional counselling and emotional support for patients and their families.

Specialized Nutrition

A dedicated oncology dietitian for personalised nutrition during chemotherapy, after surgery and throughout recovery.

Recovery & Rehabilitation

Post-treatment recovery guidance and supportive care.

Lifestyle & Wellness

Long-term nutrition, lifestyle guidance and family education.

Patient Guidance

Your first visit

Before you comeCollect all reports, scans, biopsy slides and prescriptions. Book by phone, WhatsApp or the enquiry form.
At the consultationThe doctor examines you, reviews your reports and explains the diagnosis and the options.
Your planYou receive a clear treatment plan and an estimate of costs. Only the tests that are needed are advised.
Support beginsYou meet our counsellor and dietitian, and you know whom to call with questions.

For families and caregivers

We teach caregivers how to help with food, medicines and daily care at home, and when to contact the hospital.

During chemotherapy

Our dietitian plans your nutrition, and our team advises on managing side effects between cycles.

After treatment

Recovery guidance, regular check-ups and long-term lifestyle advice help you return to normal life.

FAQs

Questions patients ask us

Do I need a referral to see a doctor?

No. You can book an appointment directly.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

All medical reports, scans and biopsy slides if available, a list of current medicines, and a family member if possible.

Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. Bring or send your reports and our specialists will review them and explain their advice.

How do you help with the cost of treatment?

We explain the expected costs before treatment begins, and we advise only the investigations that are needed.

Is counselling available for family members?

Yes. Our counselling support is for patients and their families.

Who will plan my diet during treatment?

A dedicated oncology dietitian plans personalised nutrition during chemotherapy, after surgery and throughout recovery.
