FAQs

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. Bring any reports, scans or biopsy slides you have.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Photo ID, all medical reports and scans, a list of your current medicines and your insurance card if you have one.

How long is the first consultation?

Usually 30 to 45 minutes, so the doctor has time to explain everything and answer your questions.

Can I get a cost estimate in advance?

Yes. After your consultation, our finance desk gives you a written estimate for the recommended treatment.
