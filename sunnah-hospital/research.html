Cancer Research Center

Learning from every patient we treat, so that the next patient can be treated better.

Introduction

Research that begins at the bedside

Research is part of our name because it is part of how we care. The questions our doctors study come from the patients they see every day.

The Cancer Research Center at Sunnah Hospital supports careful record-keeping, review of treatment results and studies approved by the appropriate ethics committee. The aim is practical: earlier detection, better treatment decisions and better support for patients and families.

Initiatives

Where we focus

Clinical research

Studying the results of surgical and medical cancer treatment, so that our own practice keeps improving.

Cancer data & registry

Recording the types and stages of cancer we see, to understand the needs of our region.

Awareness & early detection

Educating the community about warning signs and screening, because cancer found early is easier to treat.

Details of approved research projects will be added once provided by the hospital.

Ethics & Patients

Patients come first in every study

  • Approved research only. Every study is reviewed and approved before it begins.
  • Consent is always yours. Taking part is voluntary, and your treatment is never affected if you decline.
  • Privacy is protected. Patient information used in research is kept confidential.
