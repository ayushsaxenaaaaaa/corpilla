About Sunnah Hospital

A dedicated Surgical & Medical Cancer Center

Sunnah Hospital – Cancer Research Center brings cancer surgeons, medical oncologists, radiologists, pathologists and researchers together in one institute. Every patient's case is discussed at a tumor board, so you receive one clear, evidence-based plan.

  • Multidisciplinary tumor board
  • Dedicated care navigator
  • Advanced diagnostics & PET-CT
  • Clinical trials & research
  • Day-care chemotherapy
  • Insurance & scheme support
