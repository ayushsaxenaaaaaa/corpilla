Our Unique Approach

Beyond cancer treatment

At Sunnah Hospital, we believe cancer care goes beyond medical treatment. Along with specialised Surgical & Medical Oncology, our integrated patient-support approach focuses on five areas.

Patient Care & Support

Right Guidance

Treatment navigation and help with informed decisions, including guidance to avoid unnecessary investigations and expenses.

Psychological Support

Professional counselling and emotional support for patients and their families.

Specialized Nutrition

A dedicated oncology dietitian for personalised nutrition during chemotherapy, after surgery and throughout recovery.

Recovery & Rehabilitation

Post-treatment recovery guidance and supportive care.

Lifestyle & Wellness

Long-term nutrition, lifestyle guidance and family education.

FAQs

Questions patients ask us

Do I need a referral to see a doctor?

No. You can book an appointment directly.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

All medical reports, scans and biopsy slides if available, a list of current medicines, and a family member if possible.

Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. Bring or send your reports and our specialists will review them and explain their advice.

Is counselling available for family members?

Yes. Our counselling support is for patients and their families.

Who will plan my diet during treatment?

A dedicated oncology dietitian plans personalised nutrition during chemotherapy, after surgery and throughout recovery.
