Patient Guide

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book directly. Bring any reports, scans or biopsy slides you have.

How do I get a second opinion?

Send your reports on WhatsApp or bring them to your appointment. Our tumor board reviews them, and a specialist explains the findings to you.

Do you accept insurance and government schemes?

Yes, we work with major insurers and eligible government schemes. You receive a written cost estimate before treatment starts.

Do you help international patients?

Yes. We help with medical visa letters, airport pickup, nearby accommodation and interpreters.
