Patient Guide

Questions families ask us

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No. You can book directly. Bring any reports, scans or biopsy slides you already have.

How do I get a second opinion?

Send your reports on WhatsApp or upload them when you book. Our tumor board reviews them and a specialist explains the findings to you.

Are insurance and government schemes accepted?

We work with major insurers and eligible government schemes. Our finance desk gives you a written estimate before treatment starts.

Do you help international patients?

Yes. We help with medical visa letters, airport pickup, accommodation near the hospital and interpreters.

Can family members stay with the patient?

One attendant can stay in the room. We also offer counselling and support sessions for caregivers.
