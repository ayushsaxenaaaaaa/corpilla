Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book directly. Bring any reports, scans or biopsy slides you have.

How does the 48-hour second opinion work?

Send your reports on WhatsApp. Our tumor board reviews them and a specialist calls you to explain the findings and the options.

Do you accept insurance and government schemes?

Yes. We work with major insurers, TPAs and eligible government schemes, and you get a written estimate before treatment starts.

Do you help patients from other cities or countries?

Yes. We help with travel letters, airport pickup, nearby stays and interpreters.
